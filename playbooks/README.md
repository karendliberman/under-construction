# Playbooks

Markdown, composed by `resolve_playbook` in the worker. Ben's territory.

**Only the worker reads anything in here except `registry.yaml`** (invariant 2).
The web app reads the registry to render the picker and validate a submitted
combination, and never sees playbook prose.

The layer files listed in `registry.yaml` arrive in Phase 2 (backlog 2.1–2.3),
written by Ben rather than generated. Two playbooks end to end, not ten — you
discover the shape by writing two and noticing what they share.
